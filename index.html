Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about enrollment, drop-off rules, CCAP subsidies, or teacher positions?

We are open Monday through Friday from 5:45 AM to 6:00 PM to support early-shift healthcare workers, university faculty, teachers, and regional employees. We are located at 1001 Scotts Ave, Morris, MN 56267.
A 4-Star rating is the highest quality rating given by Minnesota Parent Aware. It proves MACCC uses research-backed curriculum, conducts ongoing teacher training, supports kindergarten readiness, and maintains excellent staff-to-child safety ratios.
Yes! MACCC accepts Minnesota Child Care Assistance Program (CCAP) subsidies through Stevens County Human Services as well as Early Learning Scholarships. Use our Tuition Calculator above to estimate your co-pay or call 320-349-1880 for assistance applying.
Children must stay home if they have a fever of 100.0°F or higher, unexplained vomiting/diarrhea, or contagious rash. Children may return once they have been symptom-free and fever-free without medication for 24 consecutive hours.
We utilize the Brightwheel app! Parents receive real-time notifications for meal times, nap duration, diaper changes/potty updates, classroom photos, and direct teacher messaging right on their mobile phone.
Lead Teachers must meet Minnesota Department of Human Services (DHS) Rule 3 requirements, which typically includes a Child Development Associate (CDA), Associate's/Bachelor's degree in Early Childhood or related field, plus CPR/First Aid certification (or willingness to complete upon hire).
